TITLE: Prescription Refill Policy
CATEGORY: Pharmacy
URL: https://clinic.example.com/pharmacy/refills
UPDATED: 2025-04-05

HOW TO REQUEST A REFILL:
1. Log into the patient portal at portal.clinic.example.com.
2. Navigate to "My Medications" and select the prescription you wish to refill.
3. Click "Request Refill" and confirm your preferred pharmacy.
4. Allow 2–3 business days for processing and pharmacy notification.

CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES:
Controlled substances (Schedule II–V medications, including opioids,
benzodiazepines, and stimulants) cannot be refilled online. These require
an in-person or telehealth appointment. A new prescription is issued at
each visit in compliance with state regulations.

ANNUAL REVIEW REQUIREMENT:
All chronic medications require an annual medication review visit. Refill
requests may be declined if your last visit was more than 12 months ago.
Please schedule a routine check-up to maintain continuity of care.

PRIOR AUTHORIZATION:
Some brand-name medications require prior authorization from your insurance
company before they will be covered. Our office will submit the request,
but this process can take 3–10 business days. Generic alternatives may
be available sooner.

PHARMACY TRANSFERS:
To transfer a prescription to a different pharmacy, contact your new pharmacy
with our office phone number. They will coordinate the transfer directly with us.

EXPIRED OR LOST PRESCRIPTIONS:
Contact our office at (555) 100-2000 to request a replacement. Bring the
original prescription container if possible.

OUT-OF-STOCK MEDICATIONS:
If your pharmacy reports a shortage, contact our office and we can identify
an alternative pharmacy or discuss a therapeutic substitute with your doctor.
