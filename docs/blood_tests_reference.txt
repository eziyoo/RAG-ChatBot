TITLE: Blood Test Reference Guide — CBC and BMP
CATEGORY: Diagnostics

UPDATED: 2025-02-14

COMPLETE BLOOD COUNT (CBC):
The CBC is one of the most common blood tests ordered. It measures:

- Red Blood Cells (RBC): Carry oxygen throughout the body.
  Low RBC may indicate anemia.
- Hemoglobin (Hgb): The protein in red blood cells that carries oxygen.
  Normal range: 12–17.5 g/dL depending on age and sex.
- Hematocrit (Hct): The percentage of blood made up of red blood cells.
  Normal range: 36–50%.
- White Blood Cells (WBC): Fight infection and respond to inflammation.
  Normal range: 4,500–11,000 cells/mcL. Elevated WBC may indicate
  infection, stress, or blood disorders.
- Platelets (PLT): Help blood clot.
  Normal range: 150,000–400,000/mcL. Low platelets may increase bleeding risk.
- MCV (Mean Corpuscular Volume): Size of red blood cells.
  Helps classify type of anemia.
- Differential: Breakdown of WBC types — neutrophils, lymphocytes,
  monocytes, eosinophils, basophils.

BASIC METABOLIC PANEL (BMP):
The BMP evaluates kidney function, blood sugar, and electrolyte balance.
It includes:

- Glucose: Blood sugar level. Fasting normal: 70–99 mg/dL.
  Elevated levels may indicate diabetes or prediabetes.
- BUN (Blood Urea Nitrogen): Measures kidney waste filtration.
  Normal: 7–20 mg/dL.
- Creatinine: Kidney function marker.
  Normal: 0.6–1.2 mg/dL (men), 0.5–1.1 mg/dL (women).
- eGFR: Estimated glomerular filtration rate — calculates kidney efficiency.
- Sodium: Electrolyte balance. Normal: 136–145 mEq/L.
- Potassium: Heart and muscle function. Normal: 3.5–5.0 mEq/L.
- Calcium: Bone health and nerve function. Normal: 8.5–10.2 mg/dL.
- CO2 (Bicarbonate): Acid-base balance indicator.

RESULT TURNAROUND:
Routine results: 24–48 hours. Stat (urgent) results: 1–4 hours.
You will be notified via the patient portal or by phone.

FASTING REQUIREMENTS:
For glucose and lipid panels, fast for 8–12 hours before blood draw.
Water is permitted.
