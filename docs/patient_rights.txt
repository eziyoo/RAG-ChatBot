TITLE: Patient Rights and Responsibilities
CATEGORY: Policy
URL: https://clinic.example.com/rights
UPDATED: 2025-01-10

PATIENT RIGHTS:
You have the right to:
1. Receive respectful, considerate care regardless of race, ethnicity,
   religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or ability to pay.
2. Be informed about your diagnosis, treatment options, risks, and prognosis
   in terms you understand.
3. Participate in decisions about your healthcare, including the right to
   refuse treatment.
4. Privacy and confidentiality of your medical information, in accordance
   with HIPAA.
5. Access your medical records and request corrections.
6. Designate a healthcare proxy or power of attorney for healthcare decisions.
7. File a grievance or complaint without fear of retaliation.
8. Receive a written estimate of expected charges before non-emergency
   procedures.

PATIENT RESPONSIBILITIES:
You are responsible for:
1. Providing accurate and complete information about your health, medications,
   allergies, and medical history.
2. Following the agreed-upon treatment plan and informing your provider if
   you are unable to do so.
3. Keeping scheduled appointments or notifying us at least 24 hours in
   advance to cancel.
4. Paying your co-pay and any outstanding balances in a timely manner.
5. Treating all staff, providers, and other patients with respect.
6. Using the patient portal securely and not sharing login credentials.

COMPLAINTS & GRIEVANCES:
To file a formal complaint, contact our Patient Advocate at
advocate@clinic.example.com or call (555) 100-2003. You may also file a
complaint with your state health department or the Joint Commission.

ADVANCE DIRECTIVES:
If you have a living will or healthcare proxy designation, please provide
a copy to our office so it can be added to your medical record.
