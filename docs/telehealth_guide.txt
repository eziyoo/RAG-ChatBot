TITLE: Telehealth Onboarding Guide
CATEGORY: Telehealth
URL: https://clinic.example.com/telehealth
UPDATED: 2025-03-15

WHAT IS TELEHEALTH?
Telehealth allows you to meet with your provider via secure video call from
home. It is suitable for follow-up visits, minor illness evaluation,
prescription renewals, mental health check-ins, and chronic disease management.

NOT SUITABLE FOR TELEHEALTH:
- Chest pain, difficulty breathing, or suspected heart attack or stroke —
  call 911 immediately.
- Severe injuries, deep lacerations, or suspected broken bones —
  go to an emergency room.
- Situations requiring physical examination, blood draws, or imaging.

HOW TO JOIN A TELEHEALTH VISIT:
1. Download the patient portal app (iOS or Android) or access via web browser
   at portal.clinic.example.com.
2. Log in with your patient credentials. If you are a new patient, register
   using your date of birth and confirmation email.
3. At your scheduled appointment time, click "Start Video Visit" from
   the appointments tab.
4. Allow the app to access your camera and microphone when prompted.
5. Your provider will join within 5 minutes of the scheduled time.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:
- Stable internet connection (minimum 5 Mbps recommended).
- Device with working camera and microphone: smartphone, tablet, or computer.
- Supported browsers: Chrome, Safari, Firefox (latest versions).
- A private, quiet environment for your session.

PRIVACY & SECURITY:
All telehealth visits are conducted over an encrypted, HIPAA-compliant
platform. Sessions are not recorded. Do not use a public Wi-Fi network.

PRESCRIPTIONS FROM TELEHEALTH:
Providers can send prescriptions electronically to your pharmacy during or
after the visit. Controlled substances cannot be prescribed via telehealth
in most states.

INSURANCE & BILLING:
Most insurance plans cover telehealth visits at the same co-pay rate as
in-person visits. Confirm with your insurer before your first visit.
Self-pay telehealth: flat rate of $75 per session.

TECHNICAL ISSUES:
If you experience problems connecting, call (555) 100-2001 (telehealth
support line) at least 10 minutes before your appointment. We will switch
to a phone-only visit if needed.
