TITLE: Colonoscopy Preparation Guide
CATEGORY: Treatment Prep
URL: https://clinic.example.com/prep/colonoscopy
UPDATED: 2025-04-01

OVERVIEW:
A colonoscopy is a procedure that allows your doctor to view the inside of your
colon using a thin, flexible camera. Proper bowel preparation is essential for
a safe and accurate exam.

TWO DAYS BEFORE:
- Begin a low-fiber diet. Avoid whole grains, nuts, seeds, raw vegetables,
  and high-fiber fruits.
- Drink plenty of clear fluids throughout the day.
- Stop taking iron supplements and anti-diarrheal medications.

ONE DAY BEFORE (PREP DAY):
- Eat or drink only clear liquids all day: water, plain broth, sports drinks
  (no red or purple dye), clear juice without pulp, black coffee or tea (no milk).
- At 5:00 PM, begin the first half of your prescribed bowel prep solution.
  Follow the mixing instructions on the package. Drink one 8-oz glass every
  15 minutes until half the solution is consumed.
- Expect frequent, loose bowel movements. Stay near a bathroom.

MORNING OF PROCEDURE:
- Complete the second half of the prep solution as directed, finishing at
  least 4 hours before your procedure time.
- Take only essential medications (blood pressure, heart medications) with
  a small sip of water.
- Do NOT eat, drink, chew gum, or suck on hard candy after finishing the
  prep solution.

MEDICATIONS:
- Inform your doctor about all medications, especially blood thinners
  (warfarin, aspirin, clopidogrel) and diabetes medications.
  Dosing adjustments may be required.

ARRIVAL & SEDATION:
- Arrive 30 minutes before your scheduled procedure time.
- You WILL receive sedation. You must arrange a responsible adult to drive
  you home. You cannot drive, operate machinery, or make legal decisions
  for 24 hours after sedation.

AFTER THE PROCEDURE:
- You may feel bloated or gassy for several hours. This is normal.
- Resume a normal diet unless your doctor advises otherwise.
- Biopsy results are typically available within 5–7 business days.
