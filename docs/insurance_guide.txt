TITLE: Insurance Coverage Guide
CATEGORY: Insurance
URL: https://clinic.example.com/insurance
UPDATED: 2025-03-28

ACCEPTED INSURANCE PLANS:
We currently accept the following insurance plans:
- Blue Cross Blue Shield (all tiers including PPO and HMO)
- Aetna (commercial and Medicare Advantage)
- Cigna (individual and employer group plans)
- United Healthcare (Choice Plus and Navigate plans)
- Humana (HMO and PPO)
- Medicare Part B (Original Medicare)
- Medicaid (state-administered plans, income-eligible patients)
- Oscar Health

OUT-OF-NETWORK:
We are not in-network with Anthem, WellCare, or Molina Healthcare as of 2025.
Patients with these plans may still be seen but should expect higher out-of-pocket costs.

BILLING:
We bill your insurance directly. You are responsible for your co-pay at the time
of service. Deductibles and coinsurance amounts depend on your specific plan.
Please contact your insurer to verify your benefits before your visit.

SELF-PAY:
Self-pay patients receive a 20% discount on listed service fees. Payment plans
are available for balances over $500. Speak with our billing department at
billing@clinic.example.com.

INSURANCE CARD:
Please bring your physical insurance card to every visit. Digital copies on
your phone are also accepted.

PRIOR AUTHORIZATIONS:
Some procedures and specialist referrals require prior authorization from your
insurer. Our referral coordinator will initiate this process on your behalf.
Allow 3–5 business days.

CLAIM DISPUTES:
If you receive an unexpected bill, contact our billing department within 30 days.
We will work with your insurer to resolve discrepancies.
